## Research Experience

### Research Assistant
**Shahid Beheshti University** | Supervisor: Dr. Mahboobeh Riahi-Madvar  
*Apr 2024 – Sep 2026*
* **Lightweight Hybrid Deep Learning for Medical Time Series (CRNN-KAN):** Proposed a novel parameter-efficient CRNN-KAN architecture for robust PCG classification, integrating a CNN-BiLSTM backbone with an EfficientKAN head. Reduced parameters to 1.31M (~100x reduction vs. VGG16) while preserving clinical interpretability via Score-CAM.
* **Scalable Outlier Detection in Big Data (mCRMR-GPPO):** Developed a distributed anomaly detection framework on Apache Spark. Formulated the mCRMR subspace selection criterion with Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) and a hierarchical Gated PPO architecture.
* **Rare Association Rule Mining:** Designed an interpretable data mining pipeline to discover high-impact patterns, improving marketing campaign conversions by 5.53x.

### Research Assistant
**Shahid Beheshti University** | Supervisor: Dr. Ehsan Bahrami Samani  
*Oct 2025 – Dec 2025*
* **Probabilistic Joint Forecasting:** Engineered a Bivariate Probabilistic LSTM framework parameterizing a dynamic Student-$t$ likelihood to model heavy tails in commodity markets (Gold and Brent Crude Oil).

### Independent Co-Researcher
**Collaborative Research**  
*Jan 2026 – Present*
* **RL-WavKAN:** Co-developed an ultra-lightweight 1D Wavelet KAN for raw audio classification using a custom PPO agent (145K parameters, 0.036 ms/sample inference latency).
* **SW-KAN:** Co-authored an architecture integrating Stieltjes–Wigert $q$-orthogonal polynomials into KANs, resolving domain mismatch via an exponential-of-tanh mapping.

### Undergraduate Research Assistant
