I am an AI engineer and researcher finishing my Master of Science degree in Data Science at **[Shahid Beheshti University](https://en.sbu.ac.ir/)** (GPA: 18.29/20). Prior to this, I completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering at **[Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST)](http://www.iust.ac.ir/en)**.  Throughout my academic journey, I have achieved elite national rankings in the highly competitive Iranian University Entrance Exams, placing in the **top 0.6%** (among >160,000 applicants) for my B.Sc. admission and the **top 1.5%** (among ~15,000 applicants) for my M.Sc. admission.

**I am actively seeking fully-funded Ph.D. positions in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science starting Fall 2027.** 
*[Please find my detailed Curriculum Vitae here](/cv/)*

### Research Focus
My primary research interests span the intersections of:
* **Efficient Deep Learning & Neural Architectures:** Designing ultra-lightweight, parameter-efficient architectures—such as CRNN-KAN, RL-WavKAN, and SW-KAN—for high-stakes and edge computing environments.
* **Medical AI & Healthcare:** Formulating robust deep learning and computer vision frameworks for biomedical signals and multimodal diagnostic data (e.g., phonocardiogram classification, bone fracture segmentation, and chest X-ray vision-language reporting).
* **Deep Reinforcement Learning & Optimization:** Exploring various RL methodologies—from value-based approaches to policy optimization—to address different challenges. In my recent work, I have applied these techniques to train custom agents for dynamic neural parameter tuning and to help develop scalable pipelines for Big Data.
* **Time Series Forecasting:** Modeling dynamic cross-asset correlations, non-Gaussian heavy-tailed distributions, and extreme tail risk in complex temporal sequences via customized likelihood estimators.

### Academic & Research Background
At Shahid Beheshti University, I conduct research as a Graduate Research Assistant under the supervision of **[Dr. Mahboobeh Riahi-Madvar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=t0Ct8QoAAAAJ&hl=en)** and **[Dr. Ehsan Bahrami Samani](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=P4blMhIAAAAJ&hl=en)**. My recent efforts focus on:

1. **Lightweight Medical Signal Modeling:** Proposing parameter-efficient CRNN-KAN and 1D RL-WavKAN models that dramatically compress parameter footprints (up to 100x reduction) while maintaining superior diagnostic accuracy and interpretability.
2. **Scalable Big Data Systems:** Architecting distributed, unsupervised analytical frameworks tailored for Big Data environments, deployed on Apache Spark and leveraging locality-sensitive hashing (LSH) alongside hierarchical policy gradient filtering.
3. **Financial Time-Series:** Engineering joint probabilistic LSTM pipelines equipped with dynamic Student-$t$ likelihoods to evaluate risk calibration across volatile commodities.

Beyond algorithmic theory, I maintain a rigorous and hands-on engineering foundation across the software and systems stack. Alongside building applied AI pipelines (such as multimodal medical vision-language models and semantic search engines), my practical experience spans constructing semantic knowledge graphs (integrating Neo4j, OWL, and LLM reasoning agents), developing compiler-level toolchains (such as ANTLR4-based AST parsers for static code analysis), and architecting scalable, containerized backend infrastructures (e.g., FastAPI, Django, Docker). Rooted in my computer engineering background, my technical scope also extends to low-level systems, including OS kernel enhancements (xv6) and digital hardware design (VHDL). Complementing my research, I am passionate about academic mentorship, regularly serving as Head TA and Teaching Assistant for core graduate courses such as *Machine Learning*, *Deep Learning*, *Big Data*, and *Advanced Data Mining*.
